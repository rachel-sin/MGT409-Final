# User test script — 2-3 celiac/GF friends

Send this to each friend (text, email, whatever's easiest). Takes about 10 minutes.

---

Hey! I built a small app that ranks DC restaurants by actual evidence of celiac safety (not just "has a GF menu"), for a class project. Would you try it for a few minutes and tell me what you think? It's for real — I need honest feedback, not a favor.

**Before you open the app:** think of ONE restaurant in DC you already have a real opinion about — somewhere you've actually eaten and either felt safe or didn't. Jot down (mentally or for real):
1. The restaurant's name
2. **How long it normally takes you** to decide if a new restaurant is worth the risk — however you currently do that (checking Find Me Gluten Free, Yelp, calling ahead, asking a GF Facebook group, whatever your actual process is). Just a rough number, like "5 minutes" or "I usually don't bother and just wing it."

**Then:** open the app → [link] → search for that same restaurant (or its neighborhood) and see what it says.

**Then fill out the feedback form** → [link to /feedback] — should take 2 minutes. It asks:
- Did the app's result match what you already knew about that restaurant?
- How long did checking the app actually take you?
- Would you trust this over what you currently use?
- Anything that confused you or felt off?

That's it — thank you!

---

## What I need to collect from this (for myself, not to send to friends)

- [ ] At least 1 real quote per tester (their actual words, good or bad)
- [ ] A before/after time comparison per tester (their stated usual process time vs. the app)
- [ ] A screenshot of at least one real result they looked at
- [ ] Whether the app's verdict matched their own real experience (the strongest form of evidence — did we agree with someone who actually ate there?)
