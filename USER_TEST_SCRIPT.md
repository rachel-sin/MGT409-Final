# User test script — 2-3 celiac/GF friends

Send this to each friend (text, email, whatever's easiest). Takes about 10 minutes.

**Feedback comes back by text**, not the in-app form — paste each friend's raw reply into the chat with Claude as you get it, and it gets logged into `data/user_feedback.json` for the report from there.

---

Hey! I built an app for a class project that ranks DC restaurants by actual evidence of celiac/gluten-free safety — not just "has a GF menu," but real signals like a dedicated fryer, staff cross-contamination knowledge, and whether reviewers actually got sick or were fine. I'd love your honest feedback since you'd actually use something like this.

**Link:** [your Codespace URL]

**What to do (~10 min):**
1. Think of a restaurant you've actually eaten at and have a real opinion on — somewhere you felt safe, or somewhere that burned you. Search for it in the app.
2. See what it says. Does it match what you actually experienced?
3. Try one more search — any neighborhood/cuisine you're curious about.

**Just text me back:**
- Did the app's verdict match your real experience at that restaurant?
- How does this compare to how you normally decide if somewhere's safe (Find Me Gluten Free, Yelp, calling ahead, winging it)? Roughly how long does that normally take vs. this?
- Would you actually trust this over what you use now?
- Anything confusing, anything you'd want added?

Don't sugarcoat it — thank you!!

---

## What to capture from each reply (for the report)

- [ ] At least 1 real quote per tester (their actual words, good or bad)
- [ ] A before/after time comparison per tester (their stated usual process time vs. the app)
- [ ] A screenshot of at least one real result they looked at (ask them to send one if they don't on their own)
- [ ] Whether the app's verdict matched their own real experience (the strongest form of evidence — did we agree with someone who actually ate there?)
